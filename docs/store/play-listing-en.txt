State Control helps you shift toward calm, focus, energy or sleep.
Breathwork helps you restore balance with guided breathing.

Use each tool on its own, or bring them together to take greater control of your state, your choices and your growth.

• Five states: Boost, Sharp Focus, Calm Control, Clarity & Relax, Sleep
• Sleep: a slow rhythm to wind down at night, up to 50 minutes
• Sessions from 5 to 50 minutes
• On your phone and on your Wear OS watch, in sync, also with the screen off

<b>02 · GUIDED BREATHWORK</b>
Find your balance. Spoken guidance and haptic cues help you use your breath to find calm, focus, energy or to prepare for sleep. The interface is unlike any other breathing app: every screen, motion and sound is designed to settle you, not stimulate you.

• 15 techniques, 48 sessions
