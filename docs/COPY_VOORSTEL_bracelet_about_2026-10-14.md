## F — FAQ (src/data/faq-content.ts, categorie bracelet + design)

**F1 · How does the Smart Bead Bracelet work?**
- Oud: "…stimulates the body's nervous system directly — triggering a physiological response before the mind can interfere… Most users notice… No screens. No effort. Just results."

Choose your state in the app and the bracelet runs the session on its own — no screen needed.\n\nUntil then, you can use State Control today on your phone or smartwatch."

**F2 · Why the wrist for haptic feedback?**
- Oud: "…direct access to nerve pathways that influence the autonomic nervous system… faster, more reliable physiological responses… bypass… cognitive filtering…"
- Nieuw: "The wrist is where you can feel a gentle rhythm without looking or listening — discreet, always within reach, and easy to notice without distraction. It's also where many people already wear a watch or bracelet all day."

**F3 · Can VIBEZCORE be used multiple times per day?**
- Oud: "…The Smart Bead Bracelet can be worn throughout the entire day… Many users wear it from morning to evening…"

**F4 · Does the Smart Bead Bracelet track personal data?**
- Nieuw: "No. The Smart Bead Bracelet is designed without health sensors: it won't monitor heart rate, sleep or other health metrics. Its only job is to deliver the rhythm.\n\nIf you choose, the VIBEZCORE app can read your resting heart rate from Apple Health or Health Connect to personalise State Control — only with your permission."

**F5 · Does VIBEZCORE require a smartphone?**
- Nieuw: "Yes. The bracelet will be set up and controlled with the VIBEZCORE app for iOS and Android, where you choose your state and session. Once a session starts, the bracelet runs it on its own — your phone doesn't need to stay nearby."

**F6 · Is the bracelet a medical device?** — alleen eerste zin

**F7 · What materials are used in the bracelet?**
- Nieuw: "The Smart Bead Bracelet is designed around a compact smart pod, threaded on a strong, flexible metal core string, with beads of real gemstones and a stainless steel locking system. Bead sets will be interchangeable, so you can change its look.\n\nFinal specifications will be published on the product page before launch."

**F8 · Is the bracelet waterproof?**
- Nieuw: "No. The Smart Bead Bracelet is not designed to be waterproof. It's made for everyday wear and light, incidental moisture — remove it before swimming, showering or other prolonged water contact. Final water-resistance details will be published on the product page before launch."

**F9 · How is the bracelet charged?**
- Nieuw: "The Smart Bead Bracelet is designed to charge by resting on its charging dock — POGO-pin contacts connect automatically, no cable to plug in. Battery life and charging time will be confirmed on the product page before launch."
