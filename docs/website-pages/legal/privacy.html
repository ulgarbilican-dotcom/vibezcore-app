Mobile App Data

When you use the VIBEZCORE mobile app, we process the following — most of it stays locally on your device. Only authentication tokens, purchase receipts, anonymous play counts (which audio session was played, linked to a random identifier — not to you or your account), crash reports (on by default; you can switch them off in Settings) and — only when you are signed in — the Backup & Sync data described below are transmitted to our servers or processors.

Random device identifier Authentication token IAP receipts (Apple / Google) Listening progress Favourites & follows Session history (Breathwork & State Control) Reminder schedule Resting heart rate (optional, State Control — entered or read with the camera)

We do not collect your IMEI, MAC address or biometric identifiers, and no health data is read from the Smart Bead Bracelet, your phone or your smartwatch. If you enter your resting heart rate for State Control, or read it with your phone's camera and flash on your fingertip, the number is used to set the pace of your rhythm. Without an account it stays on your device. If you are signed in, that single number is included in your Backup & Sync data, so it follows you to a new device. It is a wellness setting, not a medical measurement. Camera frames are analysed live and are never recorded, saved or uploaded. Reminders and smartwatch settings stay on your device. You can remove your heart rate readings anytime in Profile → Resting Heart Rate.

05

Sharing of Information

VIBEZCORE does not sell your personal data — ever.

We share limited information with the following trusted service providers only when necessary to operate our services:

Apple App Store

Merchant of Record for iOS in-app purchases — handles billing, taxes, and refunds

Google Play

Merchant of Record for Android in-app purchases — handles billing, taxes, and refunds

Supabase (EU)

Authentication, account data, subscription state and your Backup & Sync data — hosted in the European Union

Bunny CDN (EU)

Audio content delivery — based in the European Union

RevenueCat

Verifies App Store and Google Play receipts to confirm your subscription status. Receives your app user ID and purchase receipts — no payment card details

Google / Apple sign-in

Optional — only if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple. We receive your email address and a sign-in token

Sentry (EU)

Crash reports — when the app crashes or hits an error, we receive a technical report (error, app version, device model and operating system) so we can fix it. No name, email address, IP address, heart rate or session content — hosted in the European Union. You can turn this off anytime in Profile → Settings → Privacy → Share crash reports

All third parties are contractually required to protect personal information and process it only for the specific purposes listed above.

06

Mobile App Permissions

The VIBEZCORE mobile app requests only the permissions strictly necessary for the features you use. You can revoke any permission at any time via your device settings.

Bluetooth

Not requested today. It will only be used to connect a Smart Bead Bracelet once it launches (Early 2027). Never used for tracking or location.

Audio playback

Required for background audio session playback when your screen is off.

Notifications

Optional — used only if you allow notifications: the session reminders you set, and notices about your access (your Day Pass ending in 1 hour, a cancelled Premium ending in 2 days, or a payment problem).

Vibration

Used for haptic guidance during Breathwork and State Control sessions on your phone.

Smartwatch

Optional — if you pair an Apple Watch or a Wear OS watch, the app sends session timing and your access status (which sessions you can use) to it so it can guide you on your wrist. No health data is read from the watch.

Camera & flash

Optional — only when you choose to read your pulse from your fingertip in State Control. Frames are analysed live on your device and never recorded, saved or uploaded. You can always enter your resting heart rate yourself instead.

Network

Required for login, content streaming, and subscription verification.

What we do NOT request

VIBEZCORE does not request access to your microphone, location, contacts, calendar or photos, and never reads or saves health data in Apple Health or Health Connect. With an Apple Watch, VIBEZCORE asks Apple Health only for permission to start a workout, so the VIBEZCORE app opens on your watch when you start a session on your iPhone. The camera is used only for the optional fingertip pulse reading. The Smart Bead Bracelet does not collect biometric or health information.
