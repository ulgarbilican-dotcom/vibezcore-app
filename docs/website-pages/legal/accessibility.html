Accessibility Statement

VIBEZCORE is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for everyone, regardless of technology, ability, or circumstances.

Contents

Last updated: October 2026

VIBEZCORE continuously works to improve the accessibility, usability, and clarity of our digital platform so that all visitors can engage with the VIBEZCORE experience with ease.

01

What Web Accessibility Means

An accessible website allows visitors with disabilities to navigate, understand, and interact with content with the same or similar ease as other users. Accessibility may be supported through assistive technologies such as:

  • Screen readers
  • Keyboard navigation
  • Voice navigation tools
  • Alternative input devices
  • Browser accessibility features

02

In the App

The VIBEZCORE app works with TalkBack, follows your font size and respects Remove animations. Sessions combine voice, visuals and vibration, so each can be followed on its own.

03

Accessibility Adjustments on This Site

The VIBEZCORE website aims to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and strives to meet Level AA accessibility standards. The following measures have been implemented:

  • Clear and structured heading hierarchy across pages
  • Logical content order to support screen readers
  • Defined language settings for the website
  • Alternative text added to images where appropriate
  • Color combinations designed to meet accessibility contrast standards
  • Reduced unnecessary motion and animations
  • Compatibility with keyboard navigation
  • Support for common screen reader technologies
  • Efforts to ensure audio and video content is accessible

Accessibility improvements are reviewed and implemented on an ongoing basis.

04

Browser & Device Compatibility

The VIBEZCORE website is designed to be compatible with modern web browsers and assistive technologies. Tested on the latest versions of:

Google Chrome Mozilla Firefox Apple Safari Microsoft Edge

Functionality may vary slightly depending on device configuration or assistive technology.

05

Third-Party Content

Some parts of the VIBEZCORE website may include content or services provided by third-party platforms such as embedded media, external tools, or social media integrations.

While we strive to ensure accessibility throughout the website, we cannot fully guarantee the accessibility standards of third-party content.

06

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. VIBEZCORE continuously evaluates and improves the website to enhance accessibility, usability, and clarity for all visitors.

We welcome feedback that helps us improve the experience.
