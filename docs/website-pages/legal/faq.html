State Control

State Control guides your state with a rhythm you feel rather than follow. Choose one of five states — Boost, Sharp Focus, Calm Control, Clarity & Relax or Sleep — and gentle taps in a heartbeat rhythm start at your own resting heart rate and settle toward that state.

Nothing to follow, nothing to watch: you simply feel it, on your phone or your smartwatch.

Each state has a recommended length, and you can choose any whole minute in its range: Boost 5–20 minutes, Sharp Focus 5–30, Calm Control 5–30, Clarity & Relax 10–45 and Sleep 10–50.

For a quick reset, Quick Chill and Quick Boost run for 5 minutes and start right away.

Your rhythm starts where your body is. VIBEZCORE uses your resting heart rate as the starting tempo — measured in the app with your phone's camera, or entered by you — and then slowly moves toward the tempo of the state you chose. Until you measure, it uses an average resting heart rate.

Yes. In Audio & Haptics you can choose haptics only, or haptics with a soft heartbeat sound. When your smartwatch takes over the vibration, the sound keeps playing on your phone or headphones.

Breathwork

Five guided breathing states — Boost, Sharp Focus, Calm Control, Clarity & Relax and Sleep — with 15 techniques between them, each built around a specific breathing rhythm. Voice, visuals and haptics carry you through every breath in real time, on your phone or your smartwatch, so you always know exactly when to inhale, hold and exhale.

This is active practice: something you consciously do, not something you passively receive.

Each state offers several session lengths, from short resets to longer, deeper sessions. Every length has a name and a reason — tap your choice to select it, tap it again to see why that length exists. There's no single "correct" duration; pick what fits the moment.

On Android, your phone can pause apps running in the background to save battery. If that happens partway through a breathwork session, the voice guidance and vibration can stop as soon as you lock your screen — even though the timer is still running.

Allowing VIBEZCORE to run unrestricted in the background (a standard Android permission, the same one apps like Spotify or WhatsApp use for calls and playback) prevents that: your session keeps going with the same voice, visuals and haptic timing whether your screen is on or locked.

It only affects battery while a session is actually running, and it's entirely optional — without it, sessions still work, but may pause if you lock your screen partway through. You can grant it the first time a session prompts you for it, by tapping Keep sessions running when locked during a breathwork session, or in your phone's settings for VIBEZCORE (Battery → Unrestricted).
