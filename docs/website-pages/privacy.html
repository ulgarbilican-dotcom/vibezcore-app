Mobile App Permissions

The VIBEZCORE mobile app requests only the permissions strictly necessary for the features you use. You can revoke any permission at any time via your device settings.

Bluetooth

Required to connect and control your Smart Bead Bracelet. Not used for tracking or location.

Audio playback

Required for background audio session playback when your screen is off.

Notifications

Optional — used only if you opt in to session reminders or completion alerts.

Network

Required for login, content streaming, and subscription verification.

VIBEZCORE does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar, photos, or health data. The Smart Bead Bracelet does not collect biometric or health information.
