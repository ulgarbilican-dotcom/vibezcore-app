Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your VIBEZCORE account and any personal data associated with it. This page explains the process, what is removed, what is retained, and how long it takes.

Last updated: June 2026

01

About This Page

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and personal data for the VIBEZCORE mobile application (iOS and Android). It forms part of the VIBEZCORE Privacy Policy.

Below you will find the request procedure, the data that will be removed, the data we are legally required to retain, and the timeframe for completion. Requests can be made at any time and are processed free of charge.

02

How to Request Deletion

Account deletion requests are submitted through our Support Center and processed manually so that we can verify ownership before any personal data is removed.

  1. Go to the VIBEZCORE Support Center.
  2. In the contact form, select Delete my VIBEZCORE account from the subject dropdown.
  3. Enter the email address linked to your VIBEZCORE account. We use this to verify that you are the account holder.
  4. In the message field, add any additional details you would like us to consider.
  5. Submit the form. We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Subscription note

Deleting your VIBEZCORE account does not automatically cancel any active subscription. Please cancel your subscription first via the Google Play Store or Apple App Store (Settings → Subscriptions) to avoid further charges, then request account deletion.

03

Data That Will Be Removed

Once your deletion request has been verified and processed, the following data is permanently removed from our active systems:

Account & profile

Your email address, login credentials, account identifier, and registration date.

Listening progress

Resume positions, last-played session, and in-app playback history.

Favourites & follows

Saved sessions, followed series, and any in-app lists you created.

Bracelet pairing

The link between your activation code and bracelet identifier, including paired-device records.

Entitlement record

The association between your account and your purchases, including subscription and bracelet bundle status.

Support correspondence

Email correspondence between you and our support team that is linked to your account.

04

Data We Are Required to Retain

A limited amount of data must be retained after account deletion to comply with legal and tax obligations. None of this data is used to identify you after your account has been removed.

Up to 7 years

Tax & invoice records

Purchase invoices and transaction records that we are legally required to retain under Belgian and EU accounting regulations. Stored by our payment processors and used only for tax reporting and statutory audit purposes.

Up to 30 days

Encrypted backups

Routine encrypted database backups are retained for a maximum of 30 days. Your data is permanently removed as these backups cycle out of rotation.

Indefinitely · fully anonymous

Aggregate analytics

Anonymous usage statistics, such as aggregate session play counts, that were never linked to your account or any identifying information. This data cannot be traced back to you.

05

Timeline

Within 5 business days: we confirm receipt of your request.

Within 30 days: your account and the personal data listed in section 03 are removed from our active systems.

Within 30 days thereafter: residual copies are removed as encrypted backups rotate out.

If additional time is required to verify your identity or to resolve a billing matter, we will inform you in writing and explain the reason for the delay.

06

Partial Deletion

You are not required to delete your entire account in order to remove specific data. Submit a request through the Support Center specifying the data you wish to have removed — for example, your listening history or favourites — and we will process the request under the same timeframe as a full deletion.

07

Questions & Data Access

If you have questions about this page, or if you would like to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you before proceeding with deletion, please contact our support team.
