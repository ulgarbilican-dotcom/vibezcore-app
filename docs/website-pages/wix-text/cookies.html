Cookie Policy

How VIBEZCORE uses cookies and similar storage technologies — and what choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how VIBEZCORE uses cookies and similar storage technologies across both the website and the mobile app. It applies to all VIBEZCORE products — the app (Breathwork, State Control and the Audio Library) and the Smart Bead Bracelet — and complements our Privacy Policy.

Short version

We use only essential, functional storage. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party analytics that profile you. No consent banner is required because we set no non-essential trackers.

01 · What Cookies & Similar Technologies Are

"Cookies" are small text files stored on your device by a website. "Local storage" and "session storage" are similar features that allow apps to save data locally on your device without sending it to a server.

VIBEZCORE relies primarily on local storage for most functionality across both products. Without an account, your session history, favourites and listening history are stored entirely on your device; with an account, they are backed up as described under Backup & Sync.

02 · What We Actually Store

Essential — required for the service to work

  • Session token — keeps you logged in
  • Listening progress — remembers where you stopped in each session
  • Play history — your journey stats and streak
  • Session status — fully-listened vs. partly-listened flags
  • Favourites and follows — series you have saved or are following

All of the above are stored locally on your device. Only when you are signed in are your listening progress, play history and favourites also backed up to your account, as described under Backup & Sync below.

Third-party — payments and crash reports

Apple App Store and Google Play handle all in-app purchases (subscriptions and the Day Pass). RevenueCat verifies the receipts, and Sentry receives technical crash reports (on by default; you can switch them off in Settings). Each follows its own privacy policy.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, advertising networks, or any other cross-site tracking technology.

03 · Cookie Consent

Because we use only essential storage required to provide the service, no cookie consent banner is required under EU law (GDPR/ePrivacy). You will not see persistent cookie pop-ups on VIBEZCORE properties.

If we ever introduce non-essential storage (we currently have no plans to), we will request your explicit consent first.

04 · Managing Your Choices

You can control all storage through your device or browser settings:

  • Web browser — clear cookies and local storage from your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site data")
  • Mobile app — clear all VIBEZCORE local data via the in-app Settings → Clear all local data option
  • Reinstall — uninstalling and reinstalling the app removes all local data

Please note: disabling essential storage will prevent core functionality such as login, listening progress, and subscription access.

05 · Local Storage & the Mobile App

The VIBEZCORE mobile app uses device-local storage (on-device app storage) — not cookies. This data lives only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless required for the service to function (e.g. authentication tokens to verify your subscription).

Without an account, Breathwork and State Control session history is stored entirely locally and never leaves your device. If you are signed in, your Breathwork history and your State Control session history are part of your Backup & Sync data (see below).

Backup & Sync (signed-in users only)

When you are signed in, VIBEZCORE keeps a backup of the following in your account so it follows you to a new phone or after a reinstall: your settings and goals (including voice and haptics choices, your experience level and which introductions you have finished), your resting heart rate, your Breathwork history, your State Control session history, your active plan, and your Audio Library data (your favourites, the sessions you listened to, and where you stopped listening). It is stored with our database provider (Supabase), is only readable by your own account, and is deleted when you delete your account. Guests are never synced — without an account everything stays on your device. Reminders are not part of this backup.

06 · Do Not Track Signals

There is no agreed industry standard for "Do Not Track" signals, and VIBEZCORE does not currently respond to them. However, because we do not use tracking technology in the first place, this has no practical impact on your privacy.

07 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically — for instance, if we introduce a new tool or change how we store data. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Material changes will also be communicated by email or in-app notification.
