Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains the conditions under which VIBEZCORE accepts returns, cancellations, and refunds for products or services purchased through the VIBEZCORE app (via the Apple App Store or Google Play) or the VIBEZCORE website. It applies to all customers worldwide and is designed to comply with applicable consumer protection laws, including EU regulations.

Different rules apply depending on the type of purchase:

  • Physical Products — such as the VIBEZCORE Smart Bead Bracelet (not on sale yet — launching Early 2027; the sections on physical products apply once orders open)
  • Digital Content or Services — such as VIBEZCORE Premium — a monthly or yearly subscription or a Day Pass (Breathwork, State Control and the Audio Library), Digital Materials, or memberships

01 · General Policy

VIBEZCORE strives to provide high-quality products and services. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may be eligible for a return or refund under the conditions outlined in this policy.

02 · Returns for Physical Products

Customers may exercise their right of withdrawal and return physical products within 14 days of receiving the item, in accordance with applicable consumer protection laws.

To exercise this right, notify VIBEZCORE in writing before the end of the 14-day period at info@vibezcore.com, including:

  • Your order number
  • The product(s) concerned
  • Your name and contact details
  • A clear statement of withdrawal

After submission, the product must be returned within 14 days. To be eligible for a refund, the product must be:

  • Unused and in its original condition
  • Returned in original packaging where possible
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase

Once received and inspected, VIBEZCORE will notify you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are processed using the original payment method.

03 · Return Shipping

Unless the product is defective, damaged, or incorrect, customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping. We recommend using a trackable shipping method. VIBEZCORE cannot be held responsible for items lost during return transit.

04 · Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a product that is defective, damaged, or incorrect, notify us within 7 days of receiving the order at info@vibezcore.com. You may be asked to provide photos to verify the issue.

If the claim is validated, VIBEZCORE may offer:

  • A replacement product
  • A full refund, including reasonable return shipping costs where applicable

05 · Digital Products and Services

Digital products and services — including VIBEZCORE Premium (Breathwork, State Control and the Audio Library) as a subscription or Day Pass, Digital Materials, Membership Access, and Streaming Content — are generally non-refundable once access has been granted. Subscriptions and Day Passes purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play follow the refund rules of that store.

Due to the nature of digital content, returns cannot be accepted once the content has been accessed or streamed.

06 · Right of Withdrawal — Digital Content

As an EU consumer, you normally have the right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days. However, for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, this right is lost once delivery has begun.

By completing your purchase, you expressly consent to the immediate delivery of the digital content and acknowledge that you lose your right of withdrawal once access begins. In-app purchases are billed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, so refund requests for them go through the Apple App Store or Google Play.

07 · Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on your payment provider or bank. VIBEZCORE is not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment processors.

08 · Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally not eligible for refunds:

  • Digital content once accessed
  • Membership services once activated
  • Products returned in used or damaged condition (unless defective upon receipt)

09 · Order Cancellations

Orders for physical products may be canceled before the order has been processed or shipped. If an order has already been shipped, the standard return procedure applies.

You can cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings; access continues until the end of the paid period. A Day Pass cannot be cancelled once purchased.

10 · Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, your order, or the VIBEZCORE platform, please submit a request through our Support Center. Our team will review your request and respond as soon as possible.
